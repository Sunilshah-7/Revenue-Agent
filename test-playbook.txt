Revenue Playbook: Mid-Market SaaS Expansion

Positioning:
Lead with business outcomes, not features. The strongest message for mid-market SaaS companies is revenue efficiency: improving conversion rates, reducing manual sales work, and helping leadership forecast pipeline with more confidence.

Target Buyer:
Primary buyers are VP Sales, CRO, RevOps leaders, and founders. They care about pipeline quality, rep productivity, sales cycle length, churn risk, and forecast accuracy.

Discovery Questions:
Ask how the team currently identifies high-intent accounts.
Ask where sales reps lose the most time during prospect research.
Ask how leadership measures revenue leakage across the funnel.
Ask whether customer expansion signals are tracked consistently.

Pain Points:
Sales teams often spend too much time researching accounts manually.
RevOps teams struggle to connect playbook guidance with live prospect context.
Leadership lacks visibility into which opportunities are worth prioritizing.
Messaging is inconsistent across reps, causing weaker buyer engagement.

Value Drivers:
Automated research can reduce manual account prep time by 40 percent.
Grounded recommendations help reps use approved messaging from internal playbooks.
A structured business case helps executives understand ROI faster.
Real-time agent output can shorten the time from account selection to outreach.

Objection Handling:
If the buyer says they already use a CRM, explain that the platform complements CRM data by turning playbooks and prospect context into actionable sales guidance.
If the buyer is worried about AI accuracy, emphasize retrieval-grounded responses from approved internal documents.
If the buyer is concerned about adoption, recommend starting with one team and one high-value sales motion.

Business Case Template:
Executive Summary: Explain the revenue problem and why now.
Opportunity: Identify the buyer segment, pain points, and potential impact.
Risks: Call out adoption, data quality, and process-change risks.
Proposed Actions: Start with playbook ingestion, pilot agent sessions, and measure rep time saved.
Success Metrics: Track research time saved, opportunity conversion, sales cycle reduction, and pipeline influenced.

Recommended Next Step:
Run a pilot with five account executives using one sales playbook and ten target accounts. Compare research time, message quality, and manager feedback before and after the pilot.
